Welcome to The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation

The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation is a charitable organization dedicated to providing shelter, education, and healthcare to vulnerable orphans and abandoned children in Abuja, Nigeria. Our mission is to empower these children to reach their full potential, guided by good values and principles, and become productive members of society.

The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation operates under the umbrella of Laylah's Initiative for Boy and Girl Child Education, a registered NGO dedicated to improving the lives of disadvantaged, less privileged children. Founded by Ambassador Dr. Laylah Ali Othman, Laylah's Initiative provides comprehensive support services, including scholarships to facilitate access to quality education, clothing, and essential items to ensure basic needs are met, and fulfillment of basic needs for overall well-being.

Our Founder

The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation was founded as the umbrella of Laylah's Initiative for Boy and Girl Child Education by Ambassador Dr. Laylah Ali Othman, a renowned philanthropist, entrepreneur, and humanitarian. She is also the:

  • CEO of L & N Kitchen
  • CEO of Superb L & N Interior and Exterior Decor Ltd
  • Director of L & N School of Interior Design

Our Mission

Empowering children to reach their full potential, guided by good values and principles, to become productive members of society.

Our Vision

Creating a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment where vulnerable children can thrive and grow.

Our Approach

We take a holistic approach to caring for vulnerable orphans and abandoned children, addressing their physical, emotional, spiritual, and educational needs within a value-based framework.

Our Objectives

I

Shelter & Basic Needs

Providing shelter, food, clothing, and healthcare to vulnerable orphans and abandoned children.

II

Quality Education

Ensuring access to quality education and vocational training to equip them with skills for a brighter future.

III

Emotional Well-being

Fostering emotional and psychological well-being through counselling and therapy.

IV

Social & Economic Empowerment

Promoting social and economic empowerment through skills training and community engagement.

Our Services

Residential Care

Providing a safe and nurturing environment for children to live and grow.

Education Support

Ensuring access to quality education and vocational training.

Healthcare Services

Providing medical care, nutrition, and hygiene services.

Counseling & Emotional Support

Offering individual and group counseling sessions, therapy, and peer support.

Vocational Training

Providing skills training in areas such as agriculture, crafts, and entrepreneurship.

Governance & Management

The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation is governed by a Board of Trustees and managed by an Executive Director. Our staff includes caregivers, education support staff, chefs, securities, and healthcare professionals.

Facilities

Our facilities include residential buildings, recreation areas, transportation, and administrative offices. We prioritize the safety and security of our children, with perimeter fencing, a gatehouse, CCTV cameras, and security personnel.

Eligibility Criteria

We provide care and support to children aged 0-5 years who are orphaned or abandoned.

Funding

The Oasis by Laylah's Way Foundation is funded by Ambassador Dr. Laylah Ali Othman, as well as through donations from individuals and corporate bodies.
